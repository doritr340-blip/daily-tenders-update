# Israeli calendar & posting sensitivity

Dates drive what is appropriate. Israeli holidays follow the **Hebrew (lunar)
calendar**, so Gregorian dates shift each year — always confirm the current
year's dates before scheduling. Never hardcode a Gregorian date for a Jewish
holiday.

## The week
- **Work week:** Sunday–Thursday. Sunday (יום ראשון) is a normal workday.
- **Weekend:** Friday (short day) + Saturday (Shabbat).
- **Shabbat:** from Friday sunset to Saturday nightfall. Many audiences are less
  active or observant; schedule key posts Sun–Thu, with Thursday/Friday-morning
  good for weekend-leisure content. Avoid pushing observant audiences to act on
  Shabbat.
- **Best general posting windows:** Sun–Thu mornings (8–10) and evenings (20–22).

## Major holidays (festive — celebratory tone welcome)
Tie-ins, greetings, and sales are appropriate. Use correct greetings:
- **Rosh Hashana** (ראש השנה) — "שנה טובה". New-year framing, fresh starts.
- **Yom Kippur** (יום כיפור) — solemn fast day. **No promotions/sales.** Country
  largely shuts down. Greeting: "גמר חתימה טובה" / "צום קל".
- **Sukkot** (סוכות) — family, outdoors, hospitality. "חג שמח".
- **Hanukkah** (חנוכה) — 8 days, light/gifts/donuts (סופגניות). Gift & sale season.
- **Tu BiShvat** — nature/trees.
- **Purim** (פורים) — costumes, fun, playful campaigns. "פורים שמח".
- **Passover / Pesach** (פסח) — spring cleaning, family seder, chametz-free.
  Big retail period. "חג פסח שמח" / "חג שמח".
- **Lag BaOmer** — bonfires.
- **Shavuot** (שבועות) — dairy foods, "חג שמח".
- **Rosh Chodesh** — minor month-start, light tie-ins only.

## Solemn / memorial days — SUPPRESS promotional tone
On these, default to **no sales, no celebratory or jokey content.** Brands either
stay silent or post a respectful, non-commercial message. Confirm with the user
before posting anything.
- **Yom HaShoah** (יום השואה) — Holocaust Remembrance Day. Siren observed.
- **Yom HaZikaron** (יום הזיכרון) — Memorial Day for fallen soldiers & terror
  victims. Highly sensitive. Sirens. **No marketing.** Respectful posts only.
- **Yom HaAtzmaut** (יום העצמאות) — Independence Day, immediately after Yom
  HaZikaron. Festive/celebratory — but mind the abrupt transition the night before.
- **Tisha B'Av** (תשעה באב) — mourning/fast day. Subdued.
- **Yom Yerushalayim** — observed variably.

## Security-period rule
During active conflict, large-scale emergencies, or national mourning, switch to
the **memorial-day default**: pause promotions, avoid celebratory tone, and check
with the user. When unsure whether current events warrant this, ask.

## Civil / seasonal hooks
Back-to-school (September), summer (חופש גדול — long school break, July–Aug),
elections periods (stay neutral unless brand is explicitly political), and
Gregorian New Year (silvester — observed by some, low-key).

> Before scheduling: state the relevant date context to the user and flag any
> conflict, rather than silently publishing into a sensitive day.
