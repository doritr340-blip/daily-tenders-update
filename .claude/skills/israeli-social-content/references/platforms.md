# Platform norms (Israel)

Format, length, and tone conventions per platform for an Israeli audience. These
are defaults — a strong brief overrides them.

## Instagram
- **Caption length:** 1–3 short lines for engagement; up to ~125 chars before the
  "more" fold matters, so front-load the hook.
- **Hook:** question, bold claim, or relatable pain in line 1 (Hebrew).
- **Hashtags:** 5–12, mixed Hebrew + English. Put them on a separate line or
  first comment. Common: #ישראל #עסקים_קטנים #המלצה plus 1–2 niche tags.
- **Emoji:** moderate, used as bullets/accent — not every word.
- **CTA:** "שמרו לפוסט", "תייגו מישהו ש...", "לינק בביו".
- **Formats:** Reels dominate reach; carousels for value/educational; Stories for
  polls, countdowns, behind-the-scenes.

## TikTok
- **Voice:** casual, fast, Gen-Z/young-millennial slang. First 2 seconds decide.
- **Caption:** very short, often a single hook line + 2–4 hashtags.
- **Hashtags:** #פוריו (fyp equivalent usage), #טיקטוקישראל, plus topical.
- **Script style:** spoken Hebrew, contractions, direct address ("תקשיבו רגע").
- **Avoid:** corporate phrasing, long setups, overt selling up front.

## Facebook
- **Audience skews older** than IG/TikTok in Israel — clearer, slightly more
  explanatory tone works.
- **Length:** can be longer; storytelling and community posts perform.
- **Hashtags:** minimal (0–3); Facebook users react less to hashtags.
- **CTA:** comments-driven ("מה דעתכם?", "ספרו לנו בתגובות"), shares, group posts.

## LinkedIn
- **Register:** professional but human Hebrew; thought-leadership, lessons,
  hiring, milestones. Many Israeli tech posts mix Hebrew with English terms.
- **Length:** medium; hook line → whitespace → story → takeaway.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5, professional (#הייטק #קריירה #שיווק).
- **Avoid:** slang, hard-sell, excessive emoji.

## WhatsApp / Telegram (Status & broadcast / channels)
- **Very short, direct, personal.** Feels like a message, not an ad.
- **One clear CTA + link.** Often time-limited ("רק היום", "עד חצות").
- **Emoji** acceptable as visual anchors. No hashtags.
- Channels: punchy headline + 1–2 lines + link.

## X (Twitter)
- **Smaller but vocal Israeli presence** — news, politics, tech, commentary.
- **Length:** one tight thought; threads for depth.
- **Hashtags:** 0–2, topical. Tone is sharper/opinionated.

## Cross-platform CTA bank (Hebrew)
- שמרו ⭐ / שתפו / תייגו חבר
- לפרטים — קישור בביו
- הירשמו עכשיו
- ספרו לנו בתגובות
- אל תפספסו — רק עד [תאריך]
